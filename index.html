Cybersecurity guide for new remote employees

Free antivirus protection for your personal work computer

Antivirus software can reduce cybersecurity risks, but it works best with updates, strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, cautious browsing, and regular backups.

Why it matters

Remote work creates new security responsibilities

Personal computers may not include the same company-managed tools or direct IT support found in a traditional workplace. That can make remote workers easier targets.

🎣

Phishing

Messages designed to trick users into revealing information or opening harmful links.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage a device, steal data, or disrupt normal use.

🔒

Ransomware

Malware that locks files or systems and demands payment for restored access.

⬇️

Unsafe downloads

Files or programs from untrusted sources that may contain hidden threats.

First line of defense

What antivirus software does

Modern antivirus tools monitor devices for suspicious activity and respond before a threat can cause further damage.

  • Detects malicious files
  • Blocks suspicious websites
  • Scans downloads
  • Monitors system activity
  • Provides real-time protection

Bitdefender Free is a strong lightweight alternative, while Avast may appeal to users who want additional features and control.

Beyond antivirus

Software alone is not enough

Good cybersecurity habits are essential because no antivirus program can prevent every unsafe decision or account compromise.

🔄

Enable updates

Keep your operating system, browser, and applications current.

🔑

Use strong passwords

Create unique passwords and use a password manager when possible.

📲

Turn on MFA

Add a second verification step to important accounts.

🔎

Inspect links and files

Avoid unexpected attachments or unknown downloads.

☁️

Back up files

Maintain secure backups to reduce the impact of ransomware or hardware failure.

Your next step

Take five minutes to improve your protection today

Confirm that antivirus protection is active, install available updates, enable multi-factor authentication, and back up important files before your next remote work session.
